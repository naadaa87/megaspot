FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Ask us by email, KakaoTalk or the inquiry form.

Can I book if I don't speak Korean?

Yes. Write to us in English, Japanese, Chinese or Korean by email, KakaoTalk or the inquiry form on this site. Some booking documents are in Korean; if anything is unclear, just ask us.

How is the price calculated?

Hourly rate × hours, plus KRW 300,000 per booking, with 50 guests included. Each guest from the 51st adds KRW 10,000. Day, All-day and All-night packages are fixed prices that include the per-booking fee. The rate depends on the day (Mon–Thu, Friday, Saturday, Sunday) and time of day; December and January have peak-season rates. See the rate card for details.

Are taxes included?

Yes — hall fees and options include VAT. Catering buffet and cocktail-bar prices exclude VAT. Companies can receive a quote that shows the supply price and VAT separately.

How do I pay?

After we confirm availability we send a quote with payment details, and your booking is confirmed once payment is complete. If you can only pay with a card issued outside Korea, or need a company invoice, tell us when you inquire and we will check the options with you.

What is the deposit for?

The KRW 150,000 deposit is paid separately from the hall fee. After the event we check basic tidying, damage or stains, and removal of anything you attached, then return it. If we need to deduct anything, we explain why and send photos.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund within 2 hours of booking or 15 days or more before the event; 80% at 8–14 days; 60% at 6–7 days; 30% at 4–5 days; no refund from 3 days before. Refunds are based on the hall fee you paid and the time we receive your cancellation.

Is setup and clean-up time included?

Yes. Setup, the event and clean-up all happen within your booked time, so book enough hours. If you would rather leave straight after the event, add the clean-up service (KRW 200,000).

Can we bring our own food and alcohol?

Yes. You may bring your own food and drinks, including alcohol. Disposable tableware sets are available (KRW 5,000 per guest). We agree the sound volume with you in advance, and late at night we keep the volume down and the windows closed.

Can you arrange catering?

Yes. We can arrange a catered buffet, a mobile cocktail bar or finger-food catering together with your booking. These are quoted separately from the hall fee.

Can 200 people really sit in Gundae 1?

The maximum (200 in Gundae 1, 350 in Gundae 2) is the total number of people allowed. How many can sit depends on the layout — lecture, classroom, banquet or standing — so we confirm it with a layout plan once we know your event.

Can I see the hall before booking?

Yes. Site visits are free. Tell us when you would like to come and we will arrange a time.

Is there parking?

Yes, about 120 spaces in the building car park (paid). Tickets start at KRW 4,000 for 1 hour; a same-day pass is KRW 30,000. You can buy tickets online with your car number.

How early or late can we start?

From 05:00 until 07:00 the next day, in one-hour units. Hours from 05:00 to 07:00 are charged at the late-night rate. For breakfast meetings, contact us about extra discounts.

Do you offer discounts for regular events?

Yes. Regular events booked four or more times in advance, breakfast meetings and partnership events can get extra discounts and support. Contact us with your plan.

Can we put up banners or decorations?

Yes. We can print banners (KRW 120,000, design included) and X-banners (KRW 70,000). In Gundae 1 the banner areas are 5,000 × 2,000 mm (wall) and 6,000 mm wide × up to 2,400 mm high (window side). Please remove anything you attach before you leave.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes, fast Wi-Fi and wired LAN, good for presentations and live streaming. We give you the connection details on the day.

Tell us about your event

Send us your date, start and end time, and number of guests. We will reply with availability, a suggested layout and a quote.

Write to us in English, Japanese, Chinese or Korean — whichever is easiest for you. We answer messages on weekdays, 10:00–19:00 Korea time. Messages sent at weekends or on public holidays are answered on the next business day.
