MEGASPOT Gundae 1

Gundae 1 — an open hall for up to 200 guests

A 190-pyeong open hall on the 2nd floor of the Hanareum Building, used by one group at a time. Apart from four concrete columns nothing blocks the view, so long tables fit with walkways to spare, and the window seats and lounge connect to the main hall. You can run talks, dinner and networking in one room.

Before you book

Time
Setup, the event and clean-up all happen within your booked time. If you need more time, tell us in advance and we will add hours at the same hourly rate.
Guests
50 guests are included in the hall fee; each guest from the 51st adds KRW 10,000. The maximum is 200 guests in total — how many can be seated depends on the layout, which we confirm with you.
Food and drink
You may bring your own food and drinks, including alcohol. We agree the sound volume with you in advance; late at night we keep the volume down and the windows closed.
Setting up
Large items and equipment come in by elevator after we check their size and route. Storage before the event and a site visit can be arranged.
Deposit
A deposit of KRW 150,000 is paid separately and returned after we check basic tidying, damage or stains, and removal of anything you attached.
