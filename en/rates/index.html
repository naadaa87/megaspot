Rate card

Rates for Gundae 1 and Gundae 2

Both halls use the same rate card. All hall and option prices include VAT. Rate card v3.3, effective 24 November 2026.

How the hall fee is calculated

Hourly booking
Hourly rate × number of hours, plus KRW 300,000 once per booking. 50 guests are included.
Packages
Day, All-day and All-night packages are fixed prices that already include the per-booking fee and 50 guests.
Extra guests
From the 51st guest, KRW 10,000 per guest — the same in peak season.
Booking hours
From 05:00 to 07:00 the next day, in one-hour units. Include setup and clean-up time. Hours from 05:00 to 07:00 and after midnight are charged at the late-night rate of your event day.

Which rate applies?

The rate depends on whether the day of your event and the following day are holidays. Holidays are Saturdays, Sundays and Korean public holidays.

Deposit and payment

  • A deposit of KRW 150,000 is paid separately from the hall fee.
  • After your event we check basic tidying, any damage or stains, and that anything attached has been removed, then return the deposit. If we need to deduct anything, we send you the reason with photos.
  • We send payment details with your quote. Your booking is confirmed when payment is complete. If you can only pay with a card issued outside Korea, or need a company invoice, tell us when you inquire.

Refunds are a percentage of the hall fee you paid, based on the time we receive your cancellation.

  • Send cancellations by KakaoTalk or email with your booking number.
  • Card payments are refunded by cancelling the approval; it usually shows within 3–7 business days, depending on the card company.
  • If MEGASPOT has to cancel, you get a full refund. If an event cannot go ahead because of a natural disaster or a government order, you can postpone without penalty or get a full refund.

Breakfast meetings, regular events and partnerships

The rate card is for one-off events. For breakfast meetings starting early in the morning, regular events booked four or more times in advance, partnership events, pop-ups, exhibitions and shoots, contact us — we can offer extra discounts and support.

Food and drink

You may bring your own food and drinks. We can also arrange a catered buffet, a mobile cocktail bar or finger-food catering; these are quoted separately from the hall fee (buffet and cocktail prices exclude VAT).

Tell us about your event

Send us your date, start and end time, and number of guests. We will reply with availability, a suggested layout and a quote.
