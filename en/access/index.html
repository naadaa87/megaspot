Getting to MEGASPOT

Gundae 1 and Gundae 2 are on the 2nd floor of the Hanareum Building, near Konkuk Univ. Station in Gwangjin-gu, Seoul.

Address

By subway

  • Konkuk Univ. Station (건대입구역) on Seoul Subway Line 2 and Line 7 is the nearest station.
  • Walking time depends on the exit you use. Tell us where you are coming from and we will send you directions.

In the building

  • Both halls are on the 2nd floor, side by side.
  • There is an elevator, and large items can come up by elevator after we check their size.

Parking

  • The building car park has about 120 spaces on the 1st floor (front and rear) and on the 4th-floor rooftop, reached by a car lift.
  • Enter through the barrier next to GS THE FRESH. If the front looks full, drive along the side of the building to the rear and the rooftop.
  • Parking is paid. Buy a ticket online with your car number before you leave; it is registered automatically once your car has entered.
