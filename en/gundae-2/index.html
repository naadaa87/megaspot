Gundae 2 — a 400-pyeong hall for up to 350 guests

Gundae 2 is on the same 2nd floor as Gundae 1, in the neighbouring unit — more than twice the size. White ceiling panels, recessed lighting and a matte grey floor make a clean, bright hall that suits any brand colour: general meetings, large seminars, year-end parties, banquets and markets that would outgrow Gundae 1.

For 20–200 guests, Gundae 1 usually fits; for more than 100 guests or when you need a lot of floor space, Gundae 2. Both are on the same floor, so one event can use both.

Before you book

Time
Setup, the event and clean-up all happen within your booked time. If you need more time, tell us in advance and we will add hours at the same hourly rate.
Guests
50 guests are included in the hall fee; each guest from the 51st adds KRW 10,000. The maximum is 350 guests in total — seating depends on the layout, which we confirm with you.
Food and drink
You may bring your own food and drinks, including alcohol, or we can arrange a catered buffet. We agree the sound volume with you in advance; late at night we keep the volume down and the windows closed.
Setting up
Large items and equipment come in by elevator after we check their size and route. We can arrange a site visit before you book.
Deposit
A deposit of KRW 150,000 is paid separately and returned after we check basic tidying, damage or stains, and removal of anything you attached.
